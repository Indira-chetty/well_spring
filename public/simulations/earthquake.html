THE 10-YEAR LEARNING LOOP

Learn which approaches work in each place.

Local teams could record the inspected damage and whether its cause is confirmed, along with pipe material, age, soil, repairs, costs and time without service. Over years, engineers and communities could compare outcomes to reduce repeat repairs and protect clean water. Savings are a goal, not a proven result.

What a real system would require: a validated seismic-hazard or scenario model, real site and pipeline maps, local ground conditions, engineering vulnerability data, expert review and local authority guidance. RiverMamba is for river flooding—not earthquakes.
